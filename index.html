👩‍💻 I aim to build AI systems that can be
trusted by clinicians.

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Clinical AI Lab at NYU Abu Dhabi, working with Dr. Farah Shamout.
🎓 I completed my PhD in Computer Vision at MBZUAI, advised by Dr. Mohammad Yaqub and Dr. Dwarikanath Mahapatra, where I worked on domain generalization and out-of-distribution robustness for medical imaging.

Before my PhD I interned at the Inception Institute of AI (G42) and worked at IBM.
🤝 I am happy to talk about collaborations at the intersection of AI and healthcare!

🔬 Research highlights

  • Spatial understanding in medical VLMs

    Benchmarks and metrics for whether models can localize findings in chest X-rays and CT volumes, not just name them.

    • Lost in Volume: The CT-SpatialVQA Benchmark MICCAI 2026, Oral
    • CheXSpatialVQA Under Review, non-archival ECCV MedFMB Workshop 2026

  • Agents for ICU decision support

    Long-context management for LLM agents that read entire ICU stays from MIMIC-IV, and multi-agent systems that combine EHR and imaging.

    • Long Context Is Not Enough: An Empirical Case for Dynamic Context Management in ICU Agents NeurIPS LCFM Workshop 2026
    • Long Context Management and Reasoning for Clinical Agentic AI: A Survey ADIA Lab Transactions 2026

  • Robustness and generalization

    Distribution shift, zero-shot transfer, and test-time adaptation in vision-language models.

    • DiMPLE: Disentangled Multi-Modal Prompt Learning ICCV 2025
    • DRiFt: Decoupling Clinical and Class-Agnostic Features MICCAI UNSURE Workshop 2025
    • MedUnA: Can language-guided unsupervised adaptation improve medical image classification using unpaired images and texts? ISBI 2025, Oral
    • XDT-CXR: Cross-Disease Transferability in Zero-Shot CXR Classification MLHC 2024

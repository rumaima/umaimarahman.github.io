Umaima Rahman

Postdoctoral Associate, Clinical AI Lab, New York University Abu Dhabi

👩‍💻 I build AI systems that can be trusted by clinicians: vision-language models that know where things are in a scan 🩻, and agents that can reason over a patient stay 🏥.

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Clinical AI Lab at NYU Abu Dhabi, working with Dr. Farah Shamout. 🎓 I completed my PhD in Computer Vision at MBZUAI, advised by Dr. Mohammad Yaqub and Dr. Dwarikanath Mahapatra, where I worked on domain generalization and out-of-distribution robustness for medical imaging.

Before my PhD I interned at the Inception Institute of AI (G42) and worked at IBM. 🤝 I am happy to talk about collaborations at the intersection of AI and healthcare!

📝 Publications

2026

NeurIPS-Workshop2026, LCFM

Long Context Is Not Enough: An Empirical Case for Dynamic Context Management and Reasoning in ICU Clinical Decision-Support Agents

Umaima Rahman, et al.

On MIMIC-IV, serialized ICU stays grow near-linearly with length of stay and spread across 1,300+ chart variables, so fixed windows and curated subsets discard most of the record. Feeding an LLM more raw context zero-shot does not improve mortality prediction, which makes the case for active context management in ICU agents.

ECCV-Workshop2026, MedFMB

CheXSpatialVQA: evaluating spatial understanding of chest X-ray vision-language models

Umaima Rahman, et al.

A spatial VQA benchmark of 4,530 radiologist-checked question-answer pairs and the CheXSpatialScore metric, used to evaluate 13 general and medical VLMs.

MICCAI2026, early accept and Oral

Lost in Volume: The CT-SpatialVQA Benchmark for Evaluating Semantic-Spatial Understanding of 3D Medical Vision-Language Models

Mashrafi Monon, Umaima Rahman, Asif Hanif, Numan Saeed, Mohammad Yaqub

9,077 clinically grounded QA pairs from 1,601 CT volumes and reports. Current 3D medical VLMs average about 34% on semantic-spatial questions, often below chance.

2025

ICCV2025

DiMPLE: Disentangled Multi-Modal Prompt Learning: Enhancing Out-of-Distribution Alignment with Invariant and Spurious Feature Separation

Umaima Rahman, Mohammad Yaqub, Dwarikanath Mahapatra

Disentangles invariant and spurious features across image and text prompts, improving out-of-distribution alignment and robustness of vision-language models.

MICCAI-Workshop2025, UNSURE

Decoupling Clinical and Class-Agnostic Features for Reliable Few-Shot Adaptation under Shift

Umaima Rahman, Raza Imam, Mohammad Yaqub, Dwarikanath Mahapatra

DRiFt separates clinically relevant features from spurious ones in image-text embeddings, improving few-shot adaptation of medical VLMs and cross-dataset generalization.

ISBI2025

Can language-guided unsupervised adaptation improve medical image classification using unpaired images and texts?

Umaima Rahman, Raza Imam, Boulbaba Ben Amor, Mohammad Yaqub, Dwarikanath Mahapatra

Uses language guidance to adapt classifiers to new medical domains without paired image-text data.

XDT-CXR: Investigating Cross-Disease Transferability in Zero-Shot Binary Classification of Chest X-Rays

Umaima Rahman, Abhishek Basu, Muhammad Uzair Khattak, Aniq Ur Rahman

Studies how well knowledge of one chest disease transfers to unseen diseases in zero-shot classification.

Umaima Rahman, Guangyi Chen, Kun Zhang
